Title: The Exceptional Lock

Leonardo Da Vinci seemed to have been overworked, tired, sleep-deprived and really really bored when he was forced to create this rather simple (for him) lock for one of his patrons, now its apparent protection is being tested.
Even if Leonardo was not in his right state of mind when crafting this lock, it is still his work of art and is a fully functioning lock - an exceptional one at that...

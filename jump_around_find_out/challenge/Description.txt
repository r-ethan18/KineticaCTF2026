Title: Jump Around Find Out

This was one of Leonardo's many unfinished works, a really clever contraption that requires its user to go on a pilgrimage through six prominent cities of Italy by jumping between them to attain what they seek.
Sadly Leonardo himself forgot how this one works as he forgot to create an accessible method of interaction with this contraption (he is pretty forgetful like that).
But, just because the grunts couldn't figure out how this one works, does not mean that an intellectual like you cannot make it work!
